Give your design a soul.

21 Grams is a family of agent skills for product designers and video editors. Each skill teaches your AI agent a working designer's taste, so what it makes stops looking like the average of the internet.

npx skills add ilbognov/21grams

The idea

In 1907 a doctor claimed that the human soul weighs 21 grams. The science didn't hold up, but the idea stuck: there's something light and hard to measure that makes a thing alive. That's the little ghost in the logo.

Design has its own 21 grams. A screen can have every component in the right place and still feel dead. What's missing is small: the spacing rhythm, the easing curve, a button that answers when you tap it, copy that sounds like a person, the one decision nobody would make by default.

Code and video agents are now very good at the first 99%. They build a working screen or a rough cut in minutes. They're also trained on everything, so left alone they produce the most likely answer: purple gradients, emoji headings, three equal cards, captions that all look the same. The work looks AI-made because it is the statistical middle.

21 Grams skills put the missing weight back. They're written by a designer, from real rules and real examples, and they make the agent check its work against them before you see it.

What makes them different

Opinionated

Every rule is a decision someone made and can defend. Nothing is generic best practice padding.

Your system

Design skills work through your project's own components and tokens. They never add a package.

Shown, not told

Each skill page has a before and after, so you can see what changes before you install.

About

Hi, I'm Ilija

I'm Ilija Bogdanovic, founding design engineer at Conquer AI. UI and UX have been my thing since I started designing, and these days I design and build with an AI agent in the loop.

That's fast, and most of the time it's good. But I kept fixing the same things by hand: radii that don't nest, buttons with no hierarchy, icons from three different sets, motion that's either missing or bouncing for no reason. The same happened with video. The edits were fine, and every one of them looked like every other one.

So I started writing my corrections down as rules, then as skills an agent can load. 21 Grams is that notebook, cleaned up and shared. Every rule in it comes from my own work, from specimens I picked, or from videos and talks I learned from.

Start

How skills work

A skill is a folder with a SKILL.md and the rules, references and scripts it needs. Your agent reads the short description up front and only loads the rest when a request matches, so installing several costs almost nothing.

Skills here are grouped into families. Design skills work on your codebase. Video skills work on footage on your machine. More families will be added as they earn their place.

Installing

Every skill page has its own install command. They all come from the same repo:

/plugin marketplace add ilbognov/21grams

After that, ask in plain words. “Uplift this settings screen” is enough for the agent to pick up 21 Grams UI.

What's inside

  • Rule zeroFixes go through your components and tokens. No new components, no new packages.
  • IconsOne style per group, line, solid or duotone, never mixed across states.
  • Principles20 UX and animation principles, from squash and stretch to Fitts's, Hick's and the Doherty threshold.
  • Hacks10 design hacks: concentric radius, alpha borders, OKLCH colour, dvh and more.
  • Craft20 animation crafts, a typography specimen guide and an unslop pass.

The sibling to UI. Where UI is about how a screen looks and moves, UX is about how a flow works: what the person is trying to do, where they get stuck and how many steps it takes.

It will ship in the same plugin as the ux skill, with its own install command and a before and after example here.

Video family

Narrating

coming to the repo

Turns a talking-head, voiceover or vlog clip into the clean kinetic caption style for Reels, TikTok and Shorts.

It analyses the footage, writes an edit plan you can read and change, then renders it. You stay in charge of the cut without doing it frame by frame.

  • CutsJump cuts on pauses and punch-in camera moves.
  • CaptionsBig lowercase cards with one glowing keyword each, in fire gradient, magenta or lemon.
  • FinishA moody grade and loud, compressed voice audio.
