## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-18

First release.


- Scaffold: `ux` skill with rule zero, the flow-audit workflow and nine rule areas (navigation, forms, states, feedback, onboarding, destructive, accessibility, flows, choice). Rules to be filled from the author's sources.
- UX rules 6–15 in `references/rules.md` (X vs back, no blank loading, scroll restoration, active-tab to top, prefetching feeds, optimistic toggles, auto-saved drafts, permissions in context, bottom sheets, exact deep links), mapped into the navigation, states, feedback, forms and onboarding area files.
- UX laws section A in `references/laws.md`: Hick's, Fitts's, Jakob's, Miller's, Doherty Threshold, Tesler's and Postel's, each with where it applies, how to apply it and what to flag, cited as A1–A7 and mapped into the choice, accessibility, navigation, feedback, flows and forms area files.
- Field rules F1–F12 in `references/field-rules.md`, learned from the first review session (a clinical dashboard): one control per job, screen actions top right, details on demand, structured popovers, accessible paths, say it once, counts in headings, true instructions, no internal language, stated scope, account and notifications placement, in-place confirmation. Added the "Growing the field rules" process to SKILL.md.
- Field rules F13–F26 from the second review session (a collaborative canvas with AI proposals): no repeated steps, no dead controls, self-saving inline edits, selection-bound toolbars, only real choices, removable everything, previewed delete scopes, safe shortcuts, meaningful cursors, zoom-independent tools, provisional AI proposals, inspectable AI status, presence, cooldowns on the control. F12 now covers fixed widths across modes and reverting after a few seconds. Every area file now has rules.
