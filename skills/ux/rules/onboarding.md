# 21g UX · Onboarding and first run

House default. When the project has its own patterns, express this through its components and flows (SKILL.md, rule zero).

## Rules
Full rule, how to apply it and what to flag: `references/rules.md`.

- **#13** Ask for notifications, photos, camera or location only when people use the related feature.

## Field rules
Learned from real passes: `references/field-rules.md`.

- **F8** Hints and help text stay true when interactions change.
- **F27** Match onboarding depth to what people already know.
- **F28** Two doors on the first screen: start and sign in.
- **F29** One question per screen, with progress and back.
- **F30** Options show, not label.
- **F31** Reflect answers back and say how long the next step takes.
- **F32** Value before the account; Apple and Google first.
- **F33** Permissions and add-ons in context, with a clear "Not now".
- **F36** Tours after the first win, always skippable.
